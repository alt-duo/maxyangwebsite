projects and achievements

here are the main things i've been working on: research, volunteering, music, and code.

public health research

I run independent studies using the NIH All of Us Research Program data, looking at how the places people live (air pollution like PM2.5, neighborhood greenness, and neighborhood deprivation) connect to health outcomes like asthma, hypertension, and age-related macular degeneration.

So far I've worked on eight scientific abstracts, posters, and manuscripts, four as first author. My first-author manuscript was accepted to the IEEE BIBM 2026 Undergraduate & High School Symposium.

georgebot

georgebot is a Discord bot I built in Python. It handles moderation commands, some basic AI responses, and fun commands like 8 ball. It was my first serious project, and the first time I built something for real users.

AYLUS National President & San Diego Branch President

AYLUS (Alliance of Youth Leaders in the United States) is a youth-run nonprofit that organizes volunteer service across the country. I started as a volunteer, served as Chair of Technology, and now lead it as National President. I also run the San Diego branch, with 180+ volunteers.

In San Diego I lead our beach cleanups at the beaches we've officially adopted (Torrey Pines and Del Mar), recycling and water-saving drives, and food bank shifts. I also founded Smiling Stars, a weekly reading class for neurodivergent elementary schoolers that has run for about three years and 100+ sessions, and I teach kids math and English both online and in person.

I received a Silver Presidential Volunteer Service Award in 2023.

web developer, the metaphysical review

I maintain the website for The Metaphysical Review, a literary organization that publishes art, poetry, and prose submissions. I handle performance improvements and post announcements.

American Protégé International Piano & Strings Competition 2023 third place winner!

Kinda surreal: the first violin competition I ever entered, I placed third and got to perform at Carnegie Hall. I was nervous, but it was one of the best experiences I've had.

research intern at IEM UCSD

In the summer of 2023, I had the honor of interning in the Institute of Engineering in Medicine at UCSD under Dr. Linda Shi. In my internship, I did lab tests and researched about a new NAV 1.8 suppressor that is not yet in the market, running tests on mouse dorsal root ganglion cells and PC-12 cells. It was a completely new experience and opened my eyes to medical research. I came back for a second summer as a group leader.
